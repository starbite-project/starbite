# macOS on the phone: how to use it

The whole touch screen works like a MacBook trackpad. The pointer stays where it is until a finger moves it; it
does not jump under your finger.

## Pointer and clicks
| You do | macOS does |
|---|---|
| Slide one finger | The pointer moves |
| Tap | Click where the pointer is |
| Tap twice | Double-click (open a file, select a word) |
| Tap, then touch again at once and slide | Drag (move a window by its title bar, select text); lift to drop |
| Rest one finger for half a second | Right-click: the context menu (new) |
| Tap with two fingers | Right-click as well |

## Scrolling and windows
| You do | macOS does |
|---|---|
| Slide two fingers | Scroll; the page follows the fingers and coasts after a flick |
| Swipe three fingers up | Mission Control: all windows |
| Swipe three fingers down | The windows of the app in front |
| Swipe three fingers left or right | The next or previous Space or full-screen app |

## Keyboard
| You do | macOS does |
|---|---|
| Tap with four fingers | Shows the on-screen keyboard; again hides it (new) |
| Swipe up quickly from the very bottom edge | Shows the keyboard |
| The key at the keyboard's top right | Hides it |
| Tap command, option or control, then a key | A shortcut: the modifier applies to the next key only |
| Tap Shift twice | Caps lock; tap it again to switch off |

Shortcuts that help on a small screen: command-Tab switches apps, command-Q quits the app in front, command-W
closes a window, command-comma opens an app's settings, Escape closes a menu.

## Good to know
- After 10 minutes without a touch macOS puts the display to sleep. The panel stays lit and looks frozen; the next
  touch wakes it (that touch does nothing else).
- The picture runs at about 30 frames a second for now.
- Not working yet: sound, network, Finder search, the moving wallpaper. Some of Apple's own apps may still not open.
- macOS restarts into the Starbite recovery by itself only on test payloads; the everyday one runs until you leave it.
