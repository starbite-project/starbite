Why it works

  • Only Starbite. No SAMSUNG, no Galaxy S9. You are holding the phone, and the menu’s status bar already says Galaxy S9. Starbite never wears another company’s logo. The panel shows the product that is booting.
  • The splash is the intro’s first frame. The word is centred on the glass capsule (720, 1380), so it never moves. It drains to glass in place, melts into the capsule, and the capsule rises into the island.
  • The star is the wallpaper’s star. It has the shader’s own shape at the loader’s starting radius. The wallpaper star crossfades in on top of it over 120 ms, with no pop and no double. Then it flies home to (1290, 232), and the sky opens from where it woke up.
  • Built for the panel. 99.2 % of pixels stay off. The fills are flat with no glow. Each mark sits on its own 16-row JPEG blocks, with two empty block rows between them. Every black block decodes to exactly 0, at 4:4:4 and at 4:2:0.
