Starbite · Splash concept A

One mark

The wordmark and its star, alone on a dark panel. Then the word turns to glass and becomes the island, and the star rises to light the wallpaper.

0.00 s
splash

Why it works

  • One mark, nothing else. No SAMSUNG and no Galaxy S9. The phone in your hand already says what it is, and Starbite never wears another company’s logo. What the panel shows is the product that is booting.
  • The splash is the intro’s first frame. The word sits exactly on the glass capsule’s centre (720, 1380), so it never has to move. It drains to glass in place, melts into the capsule and rises into the island.
  • The star is the wallpaper’s star. It is drawn with the wallpaper shader’s own shape, so the star the loader lights is pixel-for-pixel the one already on the panel. It flies to (1290, 232), blooms, and the wallpaper opens from it.
  • Two bands on pure black. The star sits 18 rows above the letters, so capture sees two bands: the star (1263–1313) and the word (1332–1429). 99.3 % of pixels are off. The fills are flat and there are no glows, so a q95 4:4:4 JPEG stays clean.

Type and colour

  • Wordmark Inter Tight 300, 128 px, −0.01 em, #FFFFFF, 403 px wide, baseline y 1427
  • Star #6FB3FF, the wallpaper core (|x|.62 + |y|.62 ≤ 1), R 27, centre (936, 1288)
  • Panel #000000 everywhere else, including the whole logo box outside the two marks
  • Intro the v7 wallpaper (night field #030611 → #010104, star wash, silk folds), Inter Tight / Inter as in the menu
