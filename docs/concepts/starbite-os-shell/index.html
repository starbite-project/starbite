Starbite OS shell

The lock screen and home screen in the boot menu's look, built from Liquid Glass. Everything on the phone works.

Try

  • Swipe up from the bottom to unlock. Let go early and it springs back.
  • Tap a vendor to open it, then a system, then slide to start. The restart is a short demo here; tap to come back.
  • Hold the flame for Emergency, the torch for the light.
  • Home: swipe sideways for page 2, tap Search, pull down top right for Control Centre, top left to lock.
  • Open Phone and Messages. Swipe up from the bar to close an app.

Port notes

  • Logical 390 × 801.7 pt; scale 1440/390 = 3.6923.
  • Springs, mass 1: unlock k320 c32, app open k380 c34, close k360 c30, sheet k420 c36, rows k300 c30.
  • Glass samples a pre-blurred copy of the wallpaper only, plus tint, rim and highlight: no live blur of other layers.
  • Layers: wallpaper, home (cached), lock (cached), one overlay (frozen backdrop). Only transforms and alpha change per frame.
  • Systems come from the catalog's visible rows, grouped like the boot menu.
